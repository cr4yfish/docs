---
title: "Share your Genie app"
description: "Choose which workspace members can open your app in Genie, send people the link to the live app, and connect a custom domain."
---

There are two things you can share: the **project in Genie** — its chat, code and settings — and the **live app** that Genie deployed for you. They are separate. Who may open the project is decided on its Sharing page; the live app runs at its own address and is opened by its link.

## Projects are private

A project in Genie is never public. A new one starts private: only its owner can open it. There is no public/private switch and no public listing of projects — the community page is gone, and nobody outside your workspace can browse or open your project.

<Note>
  Projects that were public before became private. The live apps they deployed were not touched and keep running at the same address.
</Note>

## Share a project with your workspace

A project is shared like a [Genie Agent or Tool](/workspaces/overview#share-an-item): you name the people and teams who get it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sharing">
    In the project, open **Settings → Sharing → People with access**. The line in the project's top bar that says who can see it opens the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add people or teams">
    Search for a person, a team or everyone in the workspace, and pick a level: **View** to open it, **Edit** to build and change it, **Full access** to also share, archive and delete it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Save** applies the list. Leaving the page with unsaved changes asks first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The owner always keeps Full access. Someone who can only read the list sees it without a Save button. A project that is in no workspace can only be opened by its owner — add it to a workspace under **Settings → General** to share it.

The workspace-wide switch that used to control access to all apps at once is gone, and so is the per-project member list: every project now has its own list. A project that has never been shared keeps the access it had before.

Genie AI can read and change a project's list for you, through a [confirmation card](/workspaces/overview#confirmation-cards).

## Share the live app

Every deployed app has its own address. Copy it from the preview or from your browser's address bar while viewing the live app, and send it to anyone. Opening the live app needs no Genie account.

Who can use the live app is up to the app itself: if it has sign-in, its own users and roles decide — see [Authentication](/cloud/auth). Sharing the project in Genie and sharing the live app's link don't affect each other.

## Remix is gone

Remix — copying someone else's project into your own account — has been removed, together with the **Remix** button, *Remix from here* and the **Remixable** switch. Projects can no longer be copied into another account. Projects that were created by remixing stay as they are.

## Custom domains

You can connect a custom domain to your project so your app is accessible at a URL you own — for example, `app.yourbusiness.com` — instead of the default `genie-app.de` subdomain.

Custom domain configuration is managed through **Genie Cloud**. Your project must have Genie Cloud connected before you can attach a custom domain.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Genie Cloud">
    If you have not already done so, open **Settings > Cloud** and connect Genie Cloud to your project. See [Genie Cloud overview](/cloud/overview) for instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    In your Genie Cloud dashboard, navigate to the domain settings for your project and enter the custom domain you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your DNS">
    Follow the DNS instructions provided by Genie Cloud. You will typically need to add a CNAME record pointing your domain to the Genie Cloud-provided hostname.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for propagation">
    DNS changes can take a few minutes to several hours to take effect globally. Once propagated, your app is accessible at your custom domain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once your custom domain is active, visitors who go to your Genie URL are redirected automatically, so you can share either address without breaking anything.

## Summary of sharing options

| Option | Where | Who gets in |
|---|---|---|
| The project in Genie | **Settings → Sharing** | The owner, plus the workspace members and teams on its list |
| The live app's link | The app's own address | Anyone with the link; the app's own sign-in decides the rest |
| Custom domain | Genie Cloud | The same as the live app's link, at your own address |
