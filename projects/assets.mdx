---
title: "Add images and files to your project"
description: "Drag images directly into the chat or use the upload card to add assets to your app's public folder."
---

You can add your own images, logos, icons, and other files to any Genie project. Once uploaded, they become part of your app and Genie can reference them anywhere — as a hero image, a product photo, a logo in the navbar, or any other element your design calls for.

## Drag an image into the chat

The simplest way to get a file into your app is to include it with your prompt:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop the file onto the prompt box">
    Drag any image or video file from your computer and drop it onto the chat prompt input. A preview thumbnail appears in the input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what to do with it">
    Add a message telling Genie where to use the file:
    - "Use this as the hero background image."
    - "Add this logo to the top-left of the navbar."
    - "Show this image on each product card."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the prompt">
    Genie saves the file to your app's public folder and updates the app to reference it in the right place.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also paste an image directly from your clipboard into the prompt input if you copied it from another app or a screenshot tool.
</Tip>

## Upload files without a specific instruction

To add multiple files at once and use them in separate follow-up prompts, use the upload card:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the command palette">
    Press **Cmd/Ctrl+K** to open the command palette.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for Upload assets">
    Type "upload" and select **Upload project assets** from the results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop or pick your files">
    Drag your files onto the upload card or click to pick files from your computer. Each file shows a progress bar as it uploads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the files in follow-up prompts">
    Once uploaded, reference them in follow-up prompts:
    - "Use the uploaded hero.png as the landing page background."
    - "Place the uploaded logo.svg in the top-left corner of the navbar."
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported formats

Genie accepts common image and video formats: **PNG, JPG, GIF, SVG, WebP**, and video formats such as **MP4**. Each file must be under **2 MB**.

<Warning>
  Uploaded files are placed in your app's **public folder**, which means they are accessible via a direct URL to anyone who knows the address. Do not upload confidential documents or private files using this feature.
</Warning>
