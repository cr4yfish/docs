---
title: "Multi-role apps"
description: "Build apps where different users see different things — admin dashboards, client portals, contractor views — with access controls and roles created automatically."
---

Genie can build apps where different types of users see only what they're meant to see. Describe the user types your business needs and Genie builds the access-controlled areas, creates the roles, and names them in the build summary so you know what to assign.

## Describe your roles in the prompt

Name the user types your app needs and what each one should be able to do:

```
Build a client portal with three user types:
- An admin area to manage all bookings and users
- A contractor view showing only their assigned jobs
- A client section where customers can view their own orders
```

Genie builds each area with the appropriate access controls and creates the corresponding roles automatically.

## What Genie shows in the build summary

After building a multi-role app, the "What I built" summary names the exact role strings your app uses:

> 🛡️ **Access roles** — `admin`, `contractor` and `client`, each seeing only their own area

Use these exact names when assigning roles to your users.

## Default roles

Every Genie Cloud app includes four built-in roles that are always available:

| Role | Typical use |
|------|-------------|
| `admin` | Full access, site-wide management |
| `moderator` | Content and moderation management |
| `member` | Standard authenticated user |
| `guest` | Limited or read-only access |

## Custom roles

Any role name not in the table above is created automatically when the app is built. For example, describing a `contractor` or `client` role provisions it in the role system — `has_role` checks work from the first load, no manual setup needed.

## Assigning roles to users

Once your app is live, go to **Project Settings → Users** to assign roles to individual users. The exact role names are listed in the "What I built" build summary.

<Tip>
  Not sure what roles your app uses? Open the project chat and look at the most recent "What I built" card — it lists every role Genie created.
</Tip>
