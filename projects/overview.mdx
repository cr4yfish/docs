Every app you build in Genie lives inside a **project**. When you describe what you want to create, Genie generates a fully working app — that generated app is your project. Each project has its own URL, chat history, settings, and optionally a connected cloud backend. You can have as many projects as your plan allows, and each one is managed independently.

## Creating a project

You create a project directly from the Genie home screen by typing a description of the app you want to build into the prompt input and pressing **Enter** (or clicking **Generate**). Genie reads your description, generates the app, and opens it in the project view.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Genie">
    Go to [genie.app](https://genie.app) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your app">
    Type a description of the app you want to build into the prompt field. Be as specific as you like — for example, "a task tracker for a small team with due dates and priority labels".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Press **Enter** or click the send button. Genie will start building your app. Once it finishes, you are taken directly into the project view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also start from a pre-built template instead of writing a prompt from scratch. Templates give you a working starting point you can customize with follow-up prompts.
</Tip>

## The project view

Once your project is created, the project view is where you spend most of your time. It has two main areas side by side: the **chat panel** on the left and the **live preview** on the right.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chat panel" icon="message-square">
    The chat panel is where you talk to Genie. Type follow-up instructions — "add a search bar", "change the color scheme to dark blue" — and Genie updates the app in real time. Your full conversation history is saved and visible here.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live preview" icon="monitor">
    The live preview renders your app as it actually runs. You can click through it, fill in forms, and test behavior exactly as a real user would — without leaving the project view.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Chat history

Every message you send and every response Genie gives is stored in the chat history for that project. You can scroll back through the entire conversation to review past changes, understand why something was built a certain way, or pick up where you left off after a break.

### Chat mode

Sometimes you want to ask a question instead of asking for a change — "what does this component do?", "how would I add authentication?", or "which fields should this form have?". Toggle **Chat mode** (the chat icon next to the prompt input) and Genie will answer your next message in plain language instead of rebuilding the app.

<Tip>
  Chat mode applies to your **next message only**, then turns itself off, so your following prompt builds again as usual. Use it whenever you want a quick second opinion without spending credits on a rebuild.
</Tip>

### Project URL

Each project gets a unique URL that you can use to open it directly. You can find this URL in **Settings** under the **General** tab, or simply copy it from your browser's address bar when you are viewing the live app. If your project is public, this URL works for anyone — no login required.

On paid plans you can also connect a custom domain, so your app lives at an address you own. See [Publish and share your app](/projects/visibility) for details.

## Rolling back to a previous version

Every time Genie builds your app it creates a snapshot. If a change doesn't work out, you can revert to any previous version with one click.

To roll back:

1. Open the **pipeline card** for the version you want to restore (visible in the chat history).
2. Click **Rollback to this state**.
3. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

Genie restores the code from that snapshot, rebuilds the app, and publishes the rolled-back version at your project's URL. The rollback runs as a background task — a progress indicator shows you when it is complete. You can refresh the page at any point and the status will still be there.

<Note>
  Rolling back creates a new deployment in the history — it does not delete any of the changes you made after the version you rolled back to. Your full history is always preserved.
</Note>

## What's next suggestions

After each build, Genie analyses what has been built so far and suggests what you are likely to want to add next. These suggestions appear as cards in the **Brain** panel (accessible from the sidebar) and as prompt chips in the composer when it is empty.

Each suggestion includes a short label, a ready-to-send prompt, and a brief rationale. Click **Use this prompt** to fill the composer — Genie never sends a suggestion automatically. You can dismiss any suggestion you are not interested in.

<Tip>
  Suggestions are most useful after the first version of your app is live. They reflect the gap between what you described and what was actually built, so the more you have already built, the more targeted the suggestions become.
</Tip>

## Remixing a project

If you are viewing a public project that the owner has made remixable, you will see a **Remix** button in the project view. Clicking it creates a copy of that project in your own account — including all of its generated code — so you can build on top of it with your own prompts.

If you are not signed in when you click **Remix**, Genie will redirect you to the login page first, then complete the remix once you are authenticated.

<Tip>
  To allow others to remix your own project, enable the **Remixable** toggle in **Settings > General**. See [Project settings](/projects/settings) for details.
</Tip>
