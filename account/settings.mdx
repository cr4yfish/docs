Your account settings control personal preferences that apply to your Genie experience across all workspaces and devices.

<Note>
  Personal settings now have three pages — **Account**, **Notifications** and **Plan & credits** — in the same navigation as workspace settings. Your name, contact email and avatar, once on a separate profile page, are under **Account** now (see [Account profile](/account/profile)).
</Note>

## Opening account settings

If you're inside a workspace, click the account row at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Personal settings**. Elsewhere in Genie, click your **profile picture** in the top-right corner and select **Settings**. Either way, both your workspace settings and your personal settings share the same navigation, so the other is always one click away.

## The three pages

- **Account** — your profile (photo, name, email), **Preferences** (appearance as a Light | Dark toggle, and language), **Sign-in** (your main login, linked sign-ins, sign out) and **Delete account**.
- **Notifications** — push for this device, alerts (Genie AI replies, folder sync needing attention) and email and push per workspace.
- **Plan & credits** — your plan, credits, transactions and credit batches on one page.

## Display preferences

### Dark mode

Toggle **Dark mode** on or off to switch between the light and dark Genie interface. Your choice syncs across all devices signed in to your account — switch on desktop and your mobile experience updates automatically.

<Tip>
  Dark mode is a personal preference and does not affect how your published app looks to your users.
</Tip>

## Push notifications

Genie can send you push notifications on this device — for example when a build finishes, fails, or needs your attention. Manage them from the **Notifications** section:

- **Enable notifications on this device** — turns on push notifications for the browser or device you're currently using. Your browser will ask you to confirm permission the first time.
- **Your devices** — lists every device you've enabled notifications on, so you can see where you'll be notified.
- **Send a test notification** — sends a sample notification to confirm delivery is working before you rely on it.
- **Turn off** — disables notifications for the current device only; other devices you've enabled aren't affected.

Two switches control which events notify you:

- **Notify me when Genie AI is done** — on by default. Sends a push when a Genie AI answer finishes while you've closed the tab or aren't looking at the chat. See [Get notified when Genie AI is done](/workspaces/overview#get-notified-when-genie-ai-is-done).
- **Auto** — set up on the workspace's own [Auto](/workspaces/auto) page, where you choose whether Auto pings you when it hands you work.

<Note>
  Notification permission is per-device and per-browser. If you use Genie on your phone and your laptop, enable notifications separately on each.
</Note>

## Notification inbox

Messages inside a workspace — from colleagues, from [Auto](/workspaces/auto), from a Genie Agent's notify step, or from Genie AI — land in your **inbox**. Open it from the account row at the bottom of the workspace sidebar: **Notifications** is the first entry, and your avatar carries a small red badge with the unread count while there is one.

The inbox reads like a mail client: a list on the left, the full message on the right (one pane at a time on a phone). Opening a message marks it read. Filter by **All** or **Unread**, **Mark all as read**, or press **Ask Genie AI** for help. Members with the notify permission (owners and admins by default) can press **New notification** to write a short message to chosen colleagues or everyone in the workspace.

Under **Personal settings → Notifications**, each workspace has its own row with switches for whether its notifications also reach you by **push** and by **email**. Either way, the message stays in the inbox. Genie AI can read your inbox, mark messages read, change these switches and send you a notification, and it can send one to colleagues once you confirm on a card.

## Linked sign-ins

If you sign in a new way — your company turns on single sign-on, or you use a different login method than before — and it matches the verified email on your existing account, Genie automatically links the new sign-in method to that account instead of creating a separate, empty one. You land in your normal account with everything already there, no action needed.

Account Settings shows a **Linked sign-ins** list of every way you can currently sign in, each with an **Unlink** option if you want to remove one.
