Your account settings control personal preferences that apply to your Genie experience across all workspaces and devices.

## Opening account settings

Click your **profile picture** in the top-right corner of Genie and select **Settings**. The Account Settings page opens immediately.

## Display preferences

### Dark mode

Toggle **Dark mode** on or off to switch between the light and dark Genie interface. Your choice syncs across all devices signed in to your account — switch on desktop and your mobile experience updates automatically.

<Tip>
  Dark mode is a personal preference and does not affect how your published app looks to your users.
</Tip>
