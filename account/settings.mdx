Your account settings control personal preferences that apply to your Genie experience across all workspaces and devices.

<Note>
  Looking for your name, contact email, or avatar instead? Those live on the separate [Account profile](/account/profile) page.
</Note>

## Opening account settings

Click your **profile picture** in the top-right corner of Genie and select **Settings**. The Account Settings page opens immediately.

## Display preferences

### Dark mode

Toggle **Dark mode** on or off to switch between the light and dark Genie interface. Your choice syncs across all devices signed in to your account — switch on desktop and your mobile experience updates automatically.

<Tip>
  Dark mode is a personal preference and does not affect how your published app looks to your users.
</Tip>

## Push notifications

Genie can send you push notifications on this device — for example when a build finishes, fails, or needs your attention. Manage them from the **Notifications** section:

- **Enable notifications on this device** — turns on push notifications for the browser or device you're currently using. Your browser will ask you to confirm permission the first time.
- **Your devices** — lists every device you've enabled notifications on, so you can see where you'll be notified.
- **Send a test notification** — sends a sample notification to confirm delivery is working before you rely on it.
- **Turn off** — disables notifications for the current device only; other devices you've enabled aren't affected.

<Note>
  Notification permission is per-device and per-browser. If you use Genie on your phone and your laptop, enable notifications separately on each.
</Note>
