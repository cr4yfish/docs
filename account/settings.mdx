Your account settings control personal preferences and profile information that apply to your Genie experience across all workspaces and devices.

## Opening account settings

Click your **profile picture** in the top-right corner of Genie and select **Settings**. The Account Settings page opens immediately.

## Profile

### Display name

Your display name appears to teammates in workspace member lists and activity logs. You can set or clear it at any time.

### Contact email

This is a public-facing display email — for example, shown on your workspace profile. It is separate from your login email and changing it here does not affect how you sign in.

### Profile picture

Set an image URL to use as your avatar throughout Genie. Leave it empty to use your login provider's default avatar.

## Display preferences

### Dark mode

Toggle **Dark mode** on or off to switch between the light and dark Genie interface. Your choice syncs across all devices signed in to your account — switch on desktop and your mobile experience updates automatically.

<Tip>
  Dark mode is a personal preference and does not affect how your published app looks to your users.
</Tip>

## Manage your settings with Genie AI

You can ask the Genie workspace AI to read or update your account settings for you. Open the workspace chat and try:

- *"What is my current display name and theme?"*
- *"Switch me to dark mode."*
- *"Set my display name to Ada Lovelace."*

Genie will confirm before making changes to identity fields like your name or email.
