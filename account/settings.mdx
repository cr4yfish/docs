---
title: "Account settings"
description: "Set your personal Genie preferences — including dark mode — from Account Settings. Preferences are saved to your account and follow you across all devices."
---

Account settings let you personalise how Genie looks and behaves. Changes are saved to your account in the cloud, so your preferences follow you across all browsers and devices automatically.

**To open account settings:** click your **avatar** in the bottom-left corner of any Genie page, then choose **Account → Settings**.

## Appearance

### Dark mode

Toggle **Dark mode** to switch the entire Genie interface between light and dark themes. The change takes effect instantly across the whole app.

<Tip>
Because your preference is cloud-saved, you only need to set it once. Sign in on a different device and Genie applies your chosen theme before the first page paints — no flash, no manual reset.
</Tip>

## Syncing across devices

All preferences on the Account Settings page are cloud-synced. If you change a setting on your phone and then open Genie on a desktop, the desktop reflects your latest preference automatically.
