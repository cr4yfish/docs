title: "The setup guide"
description: "A chat with Genie AI that walks you through getting a workspace connected, read and shared — and tells new people which teams they are on and what their teams want to know."
---

The **setup guide** is a chat. Genie AI walks you through getting a workspace ready, one step at a time, in the workspace chat on a dark background. Leave whenever you like and come back later — nothing has to be finished. Only **About you** comes first; Genie AI won't mark any other step before it.

Two kinds of people see it: whoever sets up a new workspace, and the people they invite. Which steps you get depends on what you may do in the workspace.

## Nobody is sent there

The guide is offered, never forced. A new account lands on [Home](/workspaces/overview#home-jump-back-in-and-for-you) like everyone else, and two things point at the guide until it's complete:

- **First steps** on Home — the guide's next step as a card with **Continue**, beside its other steps.
- **Onboarding** at the top of the sidebar, with how far along you are — for example *Onboarding 2/7*. It disappears once everything is done.

**Setup guide** in the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar is always there, for everyone, so you can change something long after setup. All three open the same chat, where you left off, on any device. Old links to the guide still work and open your workspace's guide.

## The steps

Genie AI says what each step is for, does it with you in the chat, and moves on once it's done or you put it off (*Later*). A step that needs a page of its own — signing in to a source, setting up access, a device's notifications — comes with a link to that page, and the sidebar's **Onboarding** row brings you back.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Setting up the workspace">
    For people who may manage the workspace's settings — usually the owner or an admin setting it up.

    1. **About you** — your name and, optionally, what you do.
    2. **Connect data** — what is already connected, syncing a folder, or files dropped into the chat. The Library's [Add a source](/workspaces/overview#knowledge-base) is one link away.
    3. **Reading into the ontology** — what reading your data into the [knowledge model](/workspaces/overview#knowledge-model) means, and the switch, which Genie AI offers as a confirmation card. Reading is on by default in a new workspace and runs on a paid workspace's credits only.
    4. **People and teams** — where [Set up access](/workspaces/members#set-up-access) stands and its next step, with a link to it.
    5. **What new people are asked** — every team's [onboarding questions](#team-questions).
    6. **Ask your first question** — a real question, answered from the workspace's knowledge.
    7. **Notifications** — your notification settings.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Joining a workspace">
    For everyone else — people the workspace invited.

    1. **About you** — your name.
    2. **Your teams** — which teams you are on, what each lets you do, and the questions your teams ask, if any.
    3. **Ask your first question** — answered from the workspace's knowledge.
    4. **Notifications** — your notification settings.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A step is done when the workspace says so, not because someone ticked it: **Connect data** is done once data is coming in, **Reading into the ontology** once the knowledge model has its first entry, and **People and teams** once access has been set up. So when one admin connects Google Drive, the step reads done for every other admin too. Genie AI can't tick those for you, but it can put them off. The remaining steps are done when you finish them.

## Moving through it

- **Ask along the way.** Ask what a step means — *"what is the ontology? does it cost extra?"* — and Genie AI answers briefly, then comes back to the step.
- **Finish later** in the header takes you to the workspace whenever you like. Once everything is done it reads **To the workspace**.
- **Ask from any chat.** In any workspace chat, ask *"What's left to set up?"* — Genie AI lists what's left, with a link to each step, and can put a step off or mark it done for you.

## Team questions

By default, joining a workspace asks you nothing beyond your name: **Your teams** just tells you which teams you are on and what they let you do. A team that wants to know something from the people who join it — *Which clients do you work on?* — can add **onboarding questions**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="An admin writes the questions">
    On a team's page (**Workspace settings → Teams** → the team → **Onboarding questions**), or by asking Genie AI in the setup guide's step **What new people are asked**, which goes through every team. Each team can ask a few questions; the editor shows how many are left. Removing a question also removes the answers given to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New people answer them">
    In **Your teams**, Genie AI asks each team's questions in the chat and saves what you answer. Nothing is required: answer what you like. You can change your answers later by asking Genie AI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Genie AI uses the answers">
    When you ask Genie AI something, it knows what you answered and uses it — for example, to focus on the clients you named.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Your answers are yours.** Only you can read them — not the team's admin and not the workspace owner. They are information about you for Genie AI, not a form your company collects, and they never give you access to anything. You are only asked the questions of teams you are on now; when you leave a team, its questions and your answers to them no longer apply, and leaving the workspace deletes your answers.

If two admins edit the same team's questions at the same time, the second save is refused with *Someone else changed these questions. Nothing was saved.* and offers **Load the current questions** — so nobody deletes answers to a question they haven't seen.

Genie AI can read and change a team's questions (for people who may manage the workspace's settings) and read or save your own answers. The same tools are available to [MCP clients](/workspaces/mcp-api#team-onboarding-questions).
