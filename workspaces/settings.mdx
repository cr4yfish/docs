The workspace **Settings** area is the control centre for your workspace. Owners and admins can manage everyone and everything from here. Open it by clicking **Settings** in the left sidebar footer.

<Note>
All settings pages are restricted to workspace **owners** and **admins**. Members and editors who navigate to Settings see a permission message.
</Note>

## Settings sections

## Inline toggles

Two settings live directly on the settings overview page and don't require opening a sub-page.

### Access & Permissions

When **project-level access control** is on, workspace members must be explicitly added to individual projects before they can access them. Admins and owners always have access to all projects regardless of this setting.

When it is off, all workspace members can access all projects automatically.

### Audit & Logging

Enables or disables the workspace activity log. When enabled, every significant action is recorded with a timestamp and the actor's name. The log appears in the sidebar under **Activities** and in **Settings → Activity Log**.
