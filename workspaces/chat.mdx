---
title: "Ask Genie AI about your workspace"
description: "The Genie AI assistant answers questions about your workspace, manages members and projects, checks credit usage, and continues conversations from your project chat."
---

Every Genie workspace includes a built-in AI assistant — **Genie AI** — that you can ask anything about your workspace in plain language. It knows your projects, members, credit usage, billing status, and connected integrations, and it can take actions on your behalf with a confirmation step before anything changes.

## Opening the workspace chat

You can reach the workspace chat in two ways:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Command palette" icon="terminal">
    Press **Cmd/Ctrl+K** from anywhere in the workspace. Click the chat icon next to the search input to switch into chat mode, then type your question.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dedicated chat page" icon="message-square">
    In the workspace sidebar, click **Chat** to open the full-page chat interface where your conversation history is visible and persists between sessions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What you can ask

Genie AI can answer questions and take actions across your whole workspace:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Credit and billing questions">
    - "How many credits did I use this month?"
    - "Which project is using the most credits?"
    - "How many credits does my team have left?"
    - "Show me our credit usage broken down by day for the last two weeks."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Workspace and member information">
    - "Who are the members of this workspace and what are their roles?"
    - "How many projects do we have?"
    - "Show me recent activity in this workspace."
    - "What integrations are currently connected?"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Project management">
    - "Archive the 'Prototype v1' project."
    - "Add Maria to the 'Customer Portal' project."
    - "What's the deployment status of the dashboard project?"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Workspace settings">
    - "Invite John as a Member." — generates an invite link and shares it.
    - "Enable project access control for this workspace."
    - "What plan are we on and when does it renew?"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Actions that change workspace data — such as archiving a project or inviting a member — show a confirmation card before anything is applied. Nothing happens until you confirm.
</Note>

## Continue with Genie AI

After a **Chat mode** response in your project (when Genie answered a question without building), a **Continue with Genie AI** button appears at the bottom of the response. Clicking it opens the workspace chat with the full question and answer already loaded, so you can keep the conversation going without retyping anything.

## Ask Genie AI about your todos

At the end of each build response, Genie shows a checklist of things left to do or configure in your app. An **Ask Genie AI** button appears under that list. Clicking it opens the workspace chat with a prompt automatically drafted from your open todos, so you can discuss next steps or ask for guidance on what to tackle first.
