title: "Genie on WhatsApp, Slack, and Teams"

---

Genie runs as a bot on WhatsApp, Slack, and Microsoft Teams. Link your own account once and you can message Genie from your phone or your team chat exactly as you would in the app — with the same workspace tools and the same conversation history.

<Note>
Chat channels live in the **Library**: they are added with **Add a source → Chat apps** and listed on the **Sources** page under *Chat apps*, alongside your other connections.
</Note>

## Link your own account

This is the default way in, and any member can do it — no admin needed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sources">
    Open the **Library**, press **All sources** on the **Sources** box and find **Use Genie from your chat apps**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a code">
    Click to generate a one-time linking code, or just ask Genie AI directly: *"let me use you from WhatsApp."* The code is shown once and stays valid for 15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to the Genie bot">
    Send the code as a message to the Genie bot on the platform you want to link — WhatsApp, Slack, or Teams. Where the platform supports it, the panel links you straight to the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Genie confirms the link and replies naming your workspace. From then on, every message you send that bot is a turn in your workspace, run as you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your linked account is yours to remove at any time from the same panel on the **Sources** page. Each platform account can only be linked to one workspace at a time, and leaving a workspace also removes its link.

## Connect a channel for the whole workspace

Instead of, or alongside, personal links, an owner or admin can connect an account that belongs to the workspace itself — a support WhatsApp number customers write to, for example — answered as one fixed member or by a [Genie Agent](/workspaces/workflow-apps).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add a source">
    In the **Library**, press **+** on the **Sources** box and pick **Slack**, **Microsoft Teams** or **WhatsApp** under *Chat apps*. Each platform has its own guide; when it's done, it lists the workspace's accounts on that platform. A member who may not connect one is told who can, and where to link their own account instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a platform and connect">
    Connect a WhatsApp Business number, a Slack workspace install, or a Teams tenant. Credentials are encrypted and never shown again after saving. For Teams, install the app in your tenant, then choose **Add to Microsoft Teams** and sign in with a work account that belongs to that tenant; guest accounts can't connect it. You never type a tenant ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who answers, and who can write in">
    Choose whether the channel is answered by Genie AI or by a specific [Genie Agent](/workspaces/workflow-apps), and optionally restrict it to an allowlist of senders.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A workspace channel handled by a Genie Agent starts one run of that agent per incoming message, and replies with whatever the agent's run produces.
</Note>

Once a channel is connected, any member can also ask Genie AI to send a message through it — for example, *"text this customer on WhatsApp that their order shipped."*

## Who a message runs as

If you've linked your own account, your messages are always yours — even inside a shared inbox your workspace also connected. Otherwise, a message on a workspace channel runs as that channel's fixed member or agent, if the sender is allowed to write in. If neither applies, you get a short reply once an hour explaining how to link your account.

### Colleagues run as themselves

On a Slack channel answered by Genie AI, a colleague who never linked an account still runs as themselves, not as the person who installed the channel. This works when Slack has confirmed their email address, they belong to the Slack workspace that installed Genie, and exactly one member of your workspace has that same address verified. Guests from other organisations never match this way. Two members sharing one address match nobody.

Slack and Teams channels answered by Genie AI answer **members only**: someone who is neither linked nor matched gets the linking reply instead of an answer, so nobody reads another member's memories or chats by asking in a shared channel. If a member's Slack address differs from their Genie one, they link with a code as usual. Teams can't look up addresses yet, so on Teams every member links with a code.

When several people write in one thread, each message runs as its own author and keeps that person's own conversation history.

**Check a Slack connection.** After connecting Slack, **Check the connection** shows four things side by side, each on its own: whether Slack still accepts the app (and which permissions are missing, with **Install again**), whether a message has ever arrived (if none has, mention the app once in a channel), which channels the app is in, and — a page of members at a time — who can use Genie in Slack, and why someone can't (*Not in this Slack*, *Email not confirmed in Slack*, *Email not verified in Genie*). It only reads; nothing is changed. It needs permission to manage integrations, and Genie AI can run the same check for you.

<Note>
Owners may need to reinstall the Slack app once to grant the new permissions for reading confirmed email addresses and voice clips.
</Note>

## Replies and voice messages

Genie's answer arrives as one formatted message. Long answers are split to fit the platform's limit. Anything the app would normally show as a card is turned into plain text:

- A question with options is written out as a numbered list. Reply with your choice and Genie continues.
- Something only the app can finish, like a secure action or a password field, becomes a link to the chat.
- An agent or tool Genie built or edited comes with a link to its page.

You can also send a **voice message**: a Slack audio clip or a WhatsApp voice note is transcribed and used as your message, up to two clips per message. Transcription is billed as usual to the person the message runs as. Images and other files aren't read yet.

## Your conversation history

Everything you exchange with Genie over a chat channel lands in an ordinary workspace chat conversation, visible from **Recent chats** in the sidebar like any other — so a teammate with access can read it, and it's there the next time you write in. On WhatsApp specifically, that workspace conversation is the only history Genie has of the thread, since WhatsApp itself doesn't expose past messages.

<Tip>
Ask Genie AI things like *"let me use you from Slack"* or *"who's linked their WhatsApp to this workspace?"* — it can create a link code and manage links and channels on your behalf, the same as you can from Settings.
</Tip>
