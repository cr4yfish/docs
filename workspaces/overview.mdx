A workspace is the top-level container in Genie that brings your projects and people together in one place. Instead of working in isolation, your team shares a single environment where every project is visible, activity is tracked, and everyone works from the same context. You can belong to multiple workspaces — for example, one for your company and another for a side project — and switch between them instantly from the sidebar.

<Note>
Full team features — including member management, MCP integrations, Genie AI, and PII detection — require the **Teams** plan. You can create a workspace on any plan, but collaboration features are unlocked when you upgrade.
</Note>

## Create a workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace list">
    From anywhere in Genie, click the workspace switcher at the top of the left sidebar. Select **New Workspace** at the bottom of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    Enter a clear name for your workspace. The name appears in the sidebar, in invite links, and throughout the UI, so pick something your team will recognise immediately. You can change it later from **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a description (optional)">
    A short description helps teammates understand what the workspace is for. This step is optional — click **Continue** to skip it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and create">
    Confirm the name and description on the review screen, then click **Create**. Genie creates the workspace and takes you straight to the workspace home.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The workspace home

When you open a workspace you land on the workspace home — your team's dashboard. Signed-in users who visit the main Genie page are taken here automatically.

### Sidebar

The left sidebar is the primary navigation hub for your workspace. On desktop it starts collapsed — click your workspace logo to expand it.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Workspace switcher" icon="arrow-left-right">
    The workspace name and plan appear at the top of the sidebar. Click it to see every workspace you belong to, paginated in groups of 10. A search box appears automatically when you have more than 10 workspaces.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Overview" icon="house">
    Returns you to the workspace home from anywhere inside the workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Genie AI" icon="sparkles">
    Opens the workspace Genie AI assistant. Ask it to build a new project, update an existing one, or answer questions about your workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Activities" icon="activity">
    Opens the workspace-wide activity log. Only visible to admins and owners when the activity log is enabled.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Archive" icon="archive">
    Shows all archived projects in this workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" icon="settings">
    Opens workspace settings: members, access control, activity logging, integrations, and PII detection. Only visible to admins and owners.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
On mobile, the workspace switcher opens as a bottom drawer. Tap the workspace name at the top of the screen to switch workspaces or go to settings.
</Tip>

### Stats

Below the greeting, the home page shows four at-a-glance stat cards:

- **Active Projects** — the number of projects currently in this workspace.
- **Live Published** — how many projects have been published and are online.
- **Generations** — total AI generation count across all projects.
- **Contributors** — the number of workspace members.

### Project list

Beneath the stats, the home page shows all projects you have access to in a card grid. You can search by project name, or sort by **Recently edited**, **Newest**, or **Oldest**. The list refreshes automatically every 60 seconds and whenever you return to the browser tab, so it always reflects the latest state.

Each project card links directly to the project. If no projects exist yet, you will see an empty state with a **New Project** button.

### Activity log

If the activity log is enabled by an admin or owner, a recent-activity panel appears below the project list. It shows a live feed of workspace-wide events — new projects, member changes, setting updates — with relative timestamps. Click **View all** to open the full activity log page.

<Note>
The activity log must be turned on from **Settings → Audit & Logging** before it appears. Only admins and owners can enable or disable it.
</Note>

## Collaboration

When your team works on workspace projects together, Genie ensures that changes don't collide.

### Project editing lock

Only one person can send prompts to a workspace project at a time. When a team member is actively editing, other team members who open the same project see a banner above the prompt box showing who is currently editing. The prompt input is disabled until the lock becomes available.

The lock clears automatically within a few seconds after the active editor stops typing or leaves the project. At that point, the next person who wants to edit can take over immediately.

<Tip>
If you are viewing a project while someone else is editing, the banner updates in real time — you don't need to refresh the page to see when the lock is available.
</Tip>
