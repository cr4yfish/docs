---
title: "How Genie credits work: plans, limits, and usage"
description: "Credits power every generation in Genie. Learn how they are counted, what your plan includes, what happens when you run out, and how to check your balance."
---

Every time Genie builds or updates your app — whether you're creating it from scratch, refining a screen, or asking Genie to add a new feature — it consumes one or more credits. Credits are the unit of work in Genie: the more you build and iterate, the more credits you use.

## Credits per plan

Each plan comes with a fixed number of credits that renew at the start of every billing period.

| Plan | Monthly credits | Daily limit |
|------|----------------|-------------|
| Starter | 35 | 7 per day |
| Genie | 100 | None |
| Genie Plus | 250 | None |
| Genie Pro | 400 | None |
| Genie Ultra | 600 | None |

## Credit rollover

On all paid Genie plans (Genie, Genie Plus, Genie Pro, and Genie Ultra), unused credits from one billing period **roll over** into the next. If you don't use all your credits this month, they carry forward and are available alongside your next month's grant.

Rolled-over credits are spent first — oldest credits are always used before newer ones. Credits that still haven't been used after two billing cycles expire automatically.

<Note>
  The Starter plan does not include credit rollover. Unused Starter credits expire at the end of each billing period.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Credit rollover means you don't lose value when you have a quiet month. If you build a lot in one month and less in the next, your banked credits are there waiting for you.
</Tip>

## The Starter daily limit

On the Starter plan, you can use a maximum of **7 credits per day**, even though your monthly allowance is 35. This daily cap resets at midnight UTC. Once you hit the daily limit, Genie will not generate or update your app until the next day.

If you need to work without a daily cap, upgrading to any paid Genie plan removes this restriction entirely.

## What counts as a credit

Each prompt you send to Genie — asking it to create, modify, or extend your app — uses at least one credit. More complex changes that require Genie to reason across multiple parts of your app may use more. The exact number is shown before you confirm each request.

## What happens when you run out of credits

When your monthly credit balance reaches zero, Genie pauses generating and updating your app. Your existing app continues to run and stays accessible to your users — it simply cannot be changed until your credits renew or you add more.

<Warning>
  Genie does not automatically charge you when you run out of credits. Your app stops updating until you either wait for your monthly renewal or purchase a credit top-up.
</Warning>

You have two options when you run out:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Wait for renewal" icon="calendar">
    Your credits reset automatically at the start of your next billing period. No action needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Buy a top-up" icon="plus-circle">
    Add extra credits immediately on any paid plan. Top-ups don't expire and stack on top of your monthly allowance.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Checking your current usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your profile picture in the top-right corner of Genie, then select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    In the left sidebar, select **Billing**. You'll see your current plan, the number of credits remaining this period, and your renewal date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your usage history">
    Scroll down on the Billing page to see a log of recent credit usage broken down by project and generation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you're regularly running out of credits before your renewal date, consider upgrading to the next Genie tier or purchasing a top-up. The Genie Plus plan (250 credits/month) is often a good middle ground for active builders.
</Tip>
