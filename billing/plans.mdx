Genie offers four plan tiers — Starter, Genie (with four credit levels), Teams, and Enterprise. The difference between plans comes down to how many credits you get each month, how many projects you can create, and whether you can link your own domain. Every paid plan includes the full Genie feature set.

## Plan comparison

| Plan | Monthly credits | Daily limit | Projects | Price |
|------|----------------|-------------|----------|-------|
| Starter | 35 | 7 per day | Up to 3 | Free |
| Genie | 100 | None | Unlimited | €7/month |
| Genie Plus | 250 | None | Unlimited | €15/month |
| Genie Pro | 400 | None | Unlimited | €50/month |
| Genie Ultra | 600 | None | Unlimited | €100/month |
| Teams | Same as Genie levels | None | Unlimited | From €49/month |
| Enterprise | Custom | None | Unlimited | Contact us |

<Note>
Prices are shown in euros (€) and exclude tax. Genie is based in Hamburg, Germany. Annual billing is available and saves you money compared to paying month to month.
</Note>

## What each plan includes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Starter">
    Starter is free and a good way to explore Genie before committing to a paid plan.

    - **35 credits per month**, with a limit of 7 credits per day
    - **Up to 3 projects**
    - A unique Genie link for each project (e.g. `yourapp.genie.app`)
    - Genie branding displayed on your projects
    - Genie Cloud basic (hosting and database included)

    <Warning>
      Starter projects display Genie branding and cannot be connected to a custom domain. Upgrade to a paid plan to remove branding and link your own domain.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Genie">
    The Genie tier is for individuals who want a professional setup with their own domain. Choose the credit level that fits your usage.

    - **100–600 credits per month** depending on variant (no daily cap)
    - **Unlimited projects**
    - Link to your own custom domain
    - No Genie branding on your projects
    - Purchase additional credits at any time
    - Full Genie Cloud (hosting, database, auth, and storage)
    - German-language email support

    The four Genie variants — Genie, Genie Plus, Genie Pro, and Genie Ultra — are identical in features. They differ only in how many credits you receive each month.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Teams">
    Teams is designed for businesses and organisations that need shared workspaces, access control, and data compliance guarantees.

    - Everything in Genie, plus:
    - **Dedicated workspaces** with roles and permissions for your team
    - **EU data residency** — your data never leaves the EU
    - **GDPR compliant**, fully enterprise-grade
    - Your data is never used for AI training
    - Support via email, WhatsApp, phone, and Slack

    <Note>
      Teams plans are currently coming soon. You can join the waitlist from the [pricing page](https://genie.app/pricing).
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Enterprise">
    Enterprise is for large organisations with custom requirements. [Contact us](https://genie.app/contact) to discuss a tailored solution.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Upgrading or changing your plan

You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing** inside Genie. When you upgrade, you move to the new plan immediately and your billing period resets. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

<Tip>
  Annual billing is available for all paid plans and works out cheaper than paying monthly. You can switch between monthly and annual from the pricing page or your billing settings.
</Tip>
