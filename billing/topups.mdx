---
title: "Add credits to your Genie account"
description: "Top-ups let you buy additional credits whenever you need them on any paid plan. They stack on top of your monthly allowance and never expire."
---

If you run through your monthly credits before your billing period renews, you can purchase a credit top-up to keep building without waiting. Top-ups are available on all paid Genie plans and are a one-time purchase — they are not a subscription.

<Note>
  Top-ups are not available on the Starter (free) plan. Upgrade to any paid Genie plan to unlock the ability to purchase additional credits.
</Note>

## Available top-up amounts

Choose the amount that matches what you need. You can buy multiple top-ups at any time.

| Top-up size | Credits added |
|-------------|--------------|
| Small | 100 credits |
| Medium | 200 credits |
| Standard | 300 credits |
| Large | 500 credits |
| Extra large | 750 credits |
| Pro | 1,000 credits |
| Ultra | 1,500 credits |
| Max | 2,000 credits |

## How top-ups work alongside your monthly allowance

Top-up credits are added directly to your account balance and used alongside your monthly allowance. Genie draws from your total available balance — monthly credits and top-up credits together — without any distinction between the two.

<Tip>
  Top-up credits do not expire. If you buy more than you use this month, the remainder carries forward until you use it.
</Tip>

## How to purchase a top-up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your profile picture in the top-right corner of Genie, then select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    In the left sidebar, select **Billing** to open your billing overview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add credits">
    Click **Add credits** to open the top-up panel. You'll see all available top-up sizes alongside their prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount and check out">
    Select the top-up size you want and complete the purchase through Stripe. Credits are added to your account immediately after payment is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pricing

Top-up prices are shown in euros (€) and exclude tax. The exact price per credit decreases slightly at higher top-up amounts — buying a larger bundle costs less per credit than buying smaller amounts repeatedly.

Pricing is displayed live in the **Billing** panel when you click **Add credits**, so you always see the current price before committing.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do top-up credits expire?">
    No. Credits you purchase through a top-up never expire. They remain in your account until you use them, regardless of billing cycles or plan changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy a top-up if I'm on the Starter plan?">
    No. Top-ups are only available on paid plans (Genie, Genie Plus, Genie Pro, and Genie Ultra). Upgrade your plan first, then purchase a top-up if you need more credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my top-up credits if I downgrade?">
    Your unused top-up credits remain in your account after a plan change. You can continue using them on your new plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I buy more than one top-up at a time?">
    Yes. You can purchase top-ups multiple times. Each purchase adds to your total credit balance immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When should I buy a top-up versus upgrading my plan?">
    If you occasionally exceed your monthly allowance, a top-up is the most cost-effective option. If you consistently run out of credits every month, upgrading to a higher plan gives you more credits at a lower effective cost per credit than repeated top-ups.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
