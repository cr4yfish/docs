---
title: "App onboarding and access control"
description: "Add a welcome onboarding flow for new sign-ups and optionally restrict your app to approved users only."
---

Genie Cloud includes two features for controlling who can access your app and what new users see when they first arrive: **user onboarding** and **private mode**. Both are configured from the **Onboarding** tab in your project settings and can also be managed through a conversation with Genie AI in your workspace chat.

## User onboarding

When onboarding is enabled, every new user who signs up for your app sees a welcome screen before they can continue. The screen collects their display name and any extra questions you have set up, then lets them proceed. Once a user has completed onboarding, they never see it again.

This is useful any time your app needs to know something about a new user up front — their role, their team name, their preferences — before they start using it.

### Enable onboarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Onboarding tab">
    In your project settings, click the **Onboarding** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on onboarding">
    Toggle **Onboarding** on. Genie installs the onboarding flow into your app automatically on the next build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add custom questions (optional)">
    To collect extra information from new users beyond their display name, ask Genie to add questions to the onboarding page — for example: "Add a question asking users what industry they work in."
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  New users see the onboarding screen exactly once, immediately after signing up. Existing users who have already completed onboarding are not shown it again.
</Note>

## Private mode

Private mode restricts access to your app so that only approved users can get in. Visitors who are not approved see a "this app is private" message. Users who have signed up but not yet been approved see a waiting screen until an admin grants them access.

This is useful for beta programs, internal tools, or any app where you need to control who can use it.

### Enable private mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Onboarding tab">
    In your project settings, click the **Onboarding** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Private mode">
    Toggle **Private mode** on. Genie installs the access gate into your app automatically on the next build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve pending users">
    New sign-ups appear in the **Users** tab with a **Pending** status. Click **Approve** next to a user to grant them access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Pre-approve email addresses

If you already know which addresses should have access, add them to the **Auto-approved emails** list in the **Onboarding** tab. Anyone who signs up with a listed address is approved automatically — no manual step required.

## Manage through Genie AI

You can adjust onboarding settings and handle pending users by chatting with Genie AI in your workspace chat:

- "Enable private mode on my [project name] project"
- "Approve all pending users for [project name]"
- "Invite user@example.com to [project name]" — pre-approves the address and sends them an invitation email with a link to your live app
- "Show me who is waiting to be approved for [project name]"

Genie shows a confirmation card before applying any changes.

<Tip>
  The invite action pre-approves an email address **and** sends an invitation email in one step — handy when you want to onboard someone immediately without waiting for them to find the app themselves.
</Tip>

## Using both features together

Onboarding and private mode can be active at the same time. When both are on, the flow for a new user is: sign up → private-mode approval → onboarding screen (first visit only) → app.
