Genie is built for people who run their business, not for developers. You describe what you need — a customer tracker, a scheduling tool, an invoice system — and Genie builds a real, working app in seconds. Not a mockup, not a prototype: a live app with your own address that customers and colleagues can use immediately.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell Genie what you need">
    Type a plain-English description of the tool you want. One sentence is enough. Genie listens to the specifics: the columns you need, the workflow you follow, the way your business actually operates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Genie generates your app">
    Genie builds your app with everything underneath already in place — secure data storage, user logins, payment handling, and automatic backups. You don't configure any of it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it instantly">
    Your app gets its own address, secure and fast. Send the link to customers, colleagues, or partners and they can start using it right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep talking to change it">
    Type follow-up instructions to change anything. "Add a loyalty program." "Show weekly revenue." "Send a reminder by email." Your app grows with your business.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

### AI generation

Describe what you want in plain language and Genie generates the full app. You can refine it with follow-up prompts at any time — no technical knowledge required.

### Genie Cloud

Every app you build runs on Genie Cloud, which provides everything your app needs underneath:

- **Secure data storage** — your records are stored safely and backed up automatically
- **User logins** — let customers or colleagues log in to your app
- **Payment processing** — accept payments directly in your app
- **Email sending** — trigger emails from within your app
- **Automatic backups** — your data is protected without any setup

<Note>
  Genie Cloud infrastructure runs across Frankfurt (FRA), Ashburn (IAD), and Tokyo (NRT) for reliability and speed.
</Note>

### Templates

If you're not sure where to start, Genie offers ready-made templates for common use cases — portfolios, dashboards, CRMs, inventory tools, and more. Each template is a complete starting point you can customize with follow-up prompts. See [Start faster with Genie templates](/templates).

### MCP integrations

Genie supports MCP (Model Context Protocol) in two directions:

- **Connect external tools to Genie** — Link Linear, GitHub, Notion, Stripe, and more to your workspace. Genie AI can use those tools while building your app. See [Workspace integrations](/workspaces/integrations).
- **Connect Genie to your AI assistant** — Genie has its own MCP server so Claude Desktop, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible client can manage your workspaces and projects without opening the web app. See [Genie MCP server](/workspaces/mcp-server).

### Credit-based pricing

Genie uses a credit-based pricing model. Credits are consumed when you generate or update an app. You choose a plan based on how much you build, and you can top up credits any time. There are no hidden fees for running your apps — Genie Cloud hosting is included.

<Tip>
  You can start for free and try Genie before committing to a plan. No credit card required to get started.
</Tip>
